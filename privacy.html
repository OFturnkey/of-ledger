O F Turnkey · Privacy

O F Turnkey is a private work app used by O F Turnkey’s owners and site staff to keep project records: payments, tasks, schedules, site questions and site photos. Only people O F Turnkey has added to its list of users can use it.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in, Google tells the app your name and email address. The app uses your email only to check that you are on its list of users and what you are allowed to see. It does not ask for, and cannot get, access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What is stored

  • The records you add (entries, tasks, site questions, progress logs, photos and comments) are stored in a Google Sheet and Google Drive folders owned by O F Turnkey, together with your email address so everyone can see who added what.
  • If you turn on alerts, a notification code for your device is stored so alerts can reach it. Alerts are sent through Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • On your phone or computer, the app keeps you signed in and, when there is no signal, keeps the items waiting to be sent.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone outside O F Turnkey. The app runs on Google services (Apps Script, Sheets, Drive and Firebase), which store the data on O F Turnkey’s behalf.

Removal and questions

To stop using the app, to have your access removed, or with any question, email oft.projects@gmail.com.
